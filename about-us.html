Our Heritage

A Household Name in Kenyan Water Storage

Founded with a commitment to solve water scarcity across Kenyan communities, Kentainers Limited has grown into the benchmark manufacturer of rotationally moulded polyethylene tanks and infrastructure hardware.

Our flagship consumer and commercial brand, KENTANK, is celebrated as a household name in Kenya and a premium brand in water storage solutions. We manufacture every tank from quality-selected polyethylene resins processed through our rotational moulding plants in Nairobi, Kilifi, and Kisumu.

Unlike conventional fabricated tanks that suffer from weak seam joints and chemical brittleness under intense solar UV radiation, our rotational moulding process fuses polymers into a single seamless, rib-reinforced vessel with decades of maintenance-free service.

Engineering Superiority

Why Kenyans Choose KENTANK

When you invest in a water tank, you are investing in long-term daily water security. Here is what sets genuine KENTANK apart.

1. Double-Layer UV Barrier

Compounded carbon black outer layer shields against intense UV oxidation, completely blocking sunlight penetration and preventing internal algae growth.

2. Quality Polyethylene Resins

We use quality-selected polyethylene resins — no industrial regrind fillers. Our water storage tanks are designed for clean, chemical-free drinking water.

3. Ribbed Structural Rigidity

Engineered horizontal hoop ribs absorb massive hydrostatic wall stresses when the tank is filled with 24,000 litres of water, eliminating wall buckling.

4. Built for Long-Term Service

Engineered for extended service life when properly installed on a level, solid plinth with correct plumbing connections.

5. KEBS Standards Compliance

Manufactured in compliance with applicable Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) requirements for quality and safety.

6. Direct Transport Logistics

Dedicated company flatbed and crane trucks ensure your tank arrives at your construction site or farm safely without road damage.
