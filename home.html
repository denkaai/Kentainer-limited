Who we are

KENTANK is a household name in Kenya and a premium brand in water storage solutions, food storage and grain handling.

A leading manufacturer of polyethylene roto- and injection-moulded products under the KENTANK brand. The company also produces a wide range of solutions across residential, commercial, and industrial applications, including biogas digesters and off-grid sanitation systems.
